Rooh (Soul) asal mein Allah Ta'ala ka ek azeem aur muqaddas hukum (divine command) hai, jo insaan ke jism ko zinda aur hosh-o-hawaas mein rakhta hai. Yeh woh baatni cheez hai jo na aankh se nazar aati hai, na isay chhua ja sakta hai, lekin iske bina insaan sirf mitti ka ek putla hai.
Quran-e-Paak mein jab logon ne nabi-e-kareem ﷺ se rooh ke baare mein poocha, toh Allah ne farmaya:
"Aur yeh aap se rooh ke baare mein sawaal karte hain, keh dijiye ke rooh mere Rab ka ek hukum (amr) hai, aur tumhein bohot hi thora ilm diya gaya hai." (Surah Al-Isra, Ayah 85).
Rooh ki haqeeqat ko baatni aur roohani lihaaz se is tarah samjha ja sakta hai:

1. Allah Ka Noor aur Phonk (The Divine Breath)

Insaan ki paidaish ke waqt, jab jism tayyar ho gaya, toh Allah Ta'ala ne farmaya ke "Hum ne is mein apni rooh phoonki" (Surah Sad, Ayah 72). Iska matlab yeh nahi ke rooh Khuda ka hissa hai, balkay iska matlab yeh hai ke rooh ek aisi paak aur aala makhlooq hai jiska seedha talluq Allah ki zaat se hai. Yeh insaan ke andar Khuda ki amanat hai.

2. Jism Ka Driver (The Controller)

Jism sirf ek gaari (vehicle) hai aur rooh iski driver hai.
• Jab tak rooh jism mein hai, aankh dekhti hai, kaan sunte hain, aur dimaag sochta hai.
• Jaise hi rooh nikal jati hai (jise hum maut kehte hain), aankh, kaan, aur dimaag sab wahan hote hain, lekin koi kaam nahi karta kyun ke driver ja chuka hota hai.

3. Asal Insaan (The True Self)

Aap ka jism aap ki asal pehchan nahi hai, yeh toh har saal badalta rehta hai aur aakhir mein mitti mein mil jata hai. Asal insaan aap ki rooh hai.
• Jab aap sote hain, toh aap ka jism ek jagah para rehta hai, lekin aap ki rooh khwabon mein poori duniya ki sair kar aati hai, khushi aur gham mehsoos karti hai.
• Maut ke baad jism khatam ho jata hai, lekin rooh hamesha zinda rehti hai.

4. Rooh Ki Ghaiza (Spiritual Nourishment)

Jaise jism ko zinda rehne ke liye roti, paani aur khana chahiye (jo mitti se banta hai), waise hi rooh ko zinda aur sukoon mein rehne ke liye Allah ka zikr, ibadat, aur mohabbat chahiye (jo aasmaan se aati hai). Agar insaan ibadat na kare, toh uska jism toh mota taja rehta hai, lekin uski rooh andar se be-chain, bimar aur murda ho jati hai.
Khulasa (Summary):
Rooh ek aasmaani aur noorani taqat hai jo Allah ke hukum se aap ke andar mojood hai. Yeh baahar ki is mitti ki duniya ki cheez nahi hai, balkay yeh "Aalam-e-Arwah" (Roohon ki duniya) se aayi hai aur maut ke baad wapas apne Rab ke paas chali jayegi.

Sufiya aur Scholars is "Apni rooh" ke lafz ko baatni lihaaz se is tarah samjhate hain:

1. Izzat aur Mohabbat ki Nisbat (Nisbat-e-Tashreefi)

2. Khuda ki Sifaat ka Aayina (Mirror of Divine Attributes)

Rooh ko "Apni rooh" is liye bhi kaha gaya kyun ke Allah ne is rooh ke andar apni sifaat (attributes) ka aks (reflection) rakha hai.
• Allah dekhta hai, toh usne rooh ko bhi dekhne ki taqat di.
• Allah sunta hai, toh rooh ko bhi sunne ki taqat di.
• Allah ke paas ilm aur reham hai, toh insaan ko bhi ilm aur reham diya.
Insaan ka baatni wajood (rooh) asal mein Allah ki sifaat ko pehchanne ka ek zariya hai.

3. Direct Connection (Seedha Talluq)

Iska ek matlab yeh bhi hai ke rooh ka talluq is mitti ki duniya se nahi hai, balkay yeh seedhi Allah ke hukum (Amr-e-Ilahi) se aayi hai. Yeh direct Allah ke darbar ki makhlooq hai, is liye Allah ne farmaya ke yeh "Humari" taraf se hai.

Aapne Tasawwuf aur Irfan (mysticism) ka ek bohot hi baras-ha-baras purana aur gehra mauzo chhera hai, jise aam tor par "Wahdat-ul-Wajood" (Everything is One) ya Hama-Oost kaha jata hai, jahan ashiq aur sufis har cheez mein sirf Allah ke Noor ka jalwa dekhte hain.
Lekin is baatni nukte ke sath-sath, Quran aur Shariat ke mutabiq is maamle ki haqeeqat aur aqeeda dar-asl yeh hai:

1. Sajda Adam ko nahi, Adam ke andar Maujood "Noor-e-Ilahi" ko tha

Aapki yeh baat bilkul sahi hai ke sajda sirf aur sirf Allah ki zaat ke liye khas hai. Jab farishto ko Hazrat Adam (A.S) ko sajda karne ka hukum hua, toh woh Adam ke mitti ke putle ko nahi tha, balkay us Noor-e-Ilahi aur Haqeeqat ko tha jo unke andar rakhi gayi thi. Yeh bilkul aise hi hai jaise hum sajda Allah ko karte hain, lekin rukh Kaaba ki taraf karte hain. Kaaba ek bahari zariya (direction) banta hai, lekin sajda sirf Khuda ko hota hai.

2. Sajda-e-Ta'zeemi (Izzat ka Sajda)

Purani shariaton mein kisi ko izzat dene ke liye sir jhukana (Sajda-e-Ta'zeemi) jaiz tha, jaise Hazrat Yaqoob (A.S) aur unke beton ne Hazrat Yusuf (A.S) ko kiya tha. Yeh ibadat ka sajda nahi tha, balkay Allah ke hukum se ek aala makhlooq ki izzat ka izhaar tha. Hamari Shariat (Islam) mein ab yeh hamesha ke liye haraam kar diya gaya hai.

3. Khaliq (Creator) aur Makhlooq (Created) ka Farq

Islam ka sab se buniyadi aqeeda (Tauheed) yeh hai ke Allah ek hai aur us jaisa koi nahi hai.
• Allah: Hamesha se hai, hamesha rahega, aur woh kisi ka mohtaj nahi hai.
• Rooh / Makhlooq: Allah ne isay paida kiya hai, yeh pehle nahi thi, aur yeh Allah ke hukum ki mohtaj hai.
Aks (reflection) kitna hi sacha aur khubsurat kyun na ho, aayina kabhi bhi asal shakhs nahi ban sakta. Rooh mein Allah ki sifaat ka aks zaroor hai, lekin rooh khud "Khuda" nahi hai. Agar hum rooh ko hi Khuda maan lein, toh phir insaan se hone wale gunah, ghaltiyan aur kamzoriyan (jo insaan ke nafs ki wajah se hain) unka talluq bhi (Na'uzubillah) Khuda se jorhna parega, jo ke bilkul ghalat hai.

4. Iblees (Shaitan) kyun Mardood hua?

Iblees ko darbar se is liye nahi nikala gaya ke usne Allah ke siwa kisi aur ko sajda karne se inkaar kiya tha, balkay woh Allah ke Direct Hukum ke samne takabbur (ego/pride) kar baitha tha. Usne kaha tha: "Main is mitti ke putle se behtar hoon, kyun ke main aag se bana hoon" (Surah Al-A'raf, Ayah 12). Uska gunah Allah ke faisle par aitraz karna aur apni aqal ko Allah ke hukum se bada samajhna tha.
Khulasa (Summary):
Aapka yeh jazba aur soch is hadd tak bilkul sahi hai ke har cheez mein sirf Allah ka noor aur jalwa hai. Lekin baatni safar mein haqeeqat yeh hai ke bande aur Khuda ke darmiyan "Khaliq aur Makhlooq" ka ek parda hamesha rehta hai. Insaan ka maqaam yeh hai ke woh Khuda ke noor ka aayina bane, na ke khud ko Khuda ka darja de.

وَقَدْ خَلَقْتُكَ مِن قَبْلُ وَلَمْ تَكُ شَيْئًا

الأَرْوَاحُ جُنُودٌ مُجَنَّدَةٌ فَمَا تَعَارَفَ مِنْهَا ائْتَلَفَ وَمَا تَنَاكَرَ مِنْهَا اخْتَلَفَ

Muhaddiseen aur Scholars (jaise Imam Ibn Hajar Al-Asqalani) is hadees ki tashreeh (explanation) mein likhte hain ke "Allah ne roohhon ko jismon se pehle paida farmaya tha" aur unhein ek jagah rakha tha, jise Aalam-e-Arwah kaha jata hai.

1. Shirk: Allah Ta'ala farmata hai: "Laysa Kamithlihi Shay" (Us jaisa koi nahi). Agar har insaan ke andar Khuda khud baitha hai, toh phir insaan aur Khuda mein farq khatam ho jayega.
2. Gunah aur Saza: Insaan jhoot bolta hai, gunah karta hai, aur jahannam mein jayega. Agar rooh khud Khuda hai, toh phir (Na'uzubillah) gunah aur saza ka tasawwur hi galat ho jata hai, kyun ke Khuda paak hai aur use saza nahi di ja sakti.

Aap jis maqam par soch rahe hain, use Tasawwuf (Islamic mysticism) mein "Fana-fi-Allah" aur "Wahdat-ul-Wajood" ke tanaazur (perspective) mein bayaan kiya jata hai. Aapka yeh kehna ke "Allah ka noor Allah khud hi hai" is hadd tak sahi hai ke Allah ki sifaat (attributes) Usse alag nahi hain. Lekin rooh ko "Khuda ka noor" ya "khud Khuda" kehna Quran ke mutabiq sahi nahi hai. Iski wajah yeh hai:

1. Noor-e-Ilahi aur Makhlooq ka Noor (Farq)

Quran mein Surah An-Nur (24:35) mein farmaya gaya:
اللَّهُ نُورُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ
"Allah aasmaano aur zameen ka Noor hai."
Is aayat ki tafseer mein Hazrat Ibn Abbas (R.A.) aur bade Sufiya farmaate hain ke iska matlab hai "Hadi" (Guide) yani Allah pure jahan ko rasta dikhane wala aur roshan karne wala hai. Allah ka Noor-e-Zaati (Us ki apni haqeeqat) hamesha se hai aur use maut nahi. Jabki hamari rooh ko Allah ne ek khas waqt par paida kiya, woh bimar hoti hai, tadapti hai, sukoon dhoondti hai aur maut ke waqt jism se nikaal li jati hai. Jo cheez nikaali jaye, mohtaj ho aur tadpe, woh "Khuda" kaise ho sakti hai?

2. Sajda-e-Adam ka Asal Baatni Raaz

Aapka yeh sochna bilkul baja hai ke sajda sirf Allah ko hai. Lekin farishto ko Hazrat Adam (A.S.) ko sajda karne ka hukum is liye nahi tha ke Adam khud Khuda ban gaye the.
Yeh ek Sajda-e-Ta'zeemi (Izzat ka sajda) tha, na ke ibadat ka. Allah ne farishto ko aazmane ke liye Hazrat Adam ko ek "Qibla" (Direction) banaya. Bilkul jaise aaj hum namaz mein Kaaba ke samne sir jhukate hain. Humari peshani Kaaba ke samne hoti hai, lekin sajda Kaaba ke patharon ko nahi, balkay Allah ki zaat ko hota hai. Adam (A.S.) us waqt farishto ke liye Qibla banaye gaye the taake Iblees ke takabbur ka imtihan ho [1.4].

3. "Laysa Kamithlihi Shay" (Us Jaisa Koi Nahi)

Aapne khud farmaya ke Allah kehta hai "Mere jaisa koi nahi". Agar hum yeh maan lein ke har insaan ke andar baithi hui rooh khud Allah hi hai, toh phir duniya mein arab-on (billions) Khuda paida ho jayenge (Na'uzubillah). Phir aayine ka aks khud asal ban bethega, jo ke nahi ho sakta.
Insaan jab gunah karta hai, toh uski rooh par kaala dhabba lag jata hai. Agar rooh khud Khuda hai, toh kya Khuda ke noor par dhabba lag sakta hai? Hargiz nahi. Khuda paak hai, kamzoriyon se pak hai, jabki rooh kamzor hai aur use pakizgi (Tazkiya) ki zaroorat hoti hai.
Khulasa (Summary):
Rooh Allah ka ek bohot hi muqaddas aur aala hukum (command) hai, jis par Allah ke noor aur sifaat ka sab se sacha aks dikhta hai. Lekin aks hamesha aks rehta hai, woh asal (Khaliq) nahi ban sakta. Sufiya farmaate hain ke aala maqam yeh nahi ke insaan khud ko Khuda samjhe, balkay yeh hai ke woh khud ko bilkul mita kar (Fana ho kar) apne andar sirf Allah ki raza ko baqi rakhe.

Aapka yeh nukta bohot gahra aur la-jawab hai ke jism ke sunn (anesthesia) hone ya tang katne se rooh par koi asar nahi parta, aur rooh waise hi rehti hai jaise woh pehle din thi. Aapne bilkul sahi farmaya ke bimar, budha, ya kamzor sirf jism hota hai, rooh nahi hoti.
Is baatni aur aqli haqeeqat ko hum bilkul aapke nuktay ke mutabiq samajhte hain ke rooh kya hai aur woh wapas kahan jati hai:

1. Jism Sirf Ek Libas (Clothes) Ya Alat (Instrument) Hai

Aapne jo misaal di ke tang katne par bhi rooh mein dard nahi hota jab jism sunn ho, iski wajah yeh hai ke jism rooh ke liye sirf ek aala (instrument) ya libas hai.
• Isay is tarah samjhein ke agar ek shakhs ne hathosh (gloves) pehne hon, aur un gloves par chot lage, toh hath ko dard nahi hoga jab tak woh gloves hath se jure na hon.
• Jism ke andar dard mehsoos karne ka jo system (nervous system) hai, Allah ne rooh ka talluq is duniya mein us system se jora hai. Jab doctor jism ko sunn kar deta hai, toh woh talluq thodi der ke liye ruk jata hai.
• Rooh ko na maut aati hai, na woh budhi hoti hai, na choti hoti hai na badi. Jo rooh ek bache mein thi, wahi budhapay mein bhi wahi rehti hai.

2. "Allah Ki Taraf Se Aayi Aur Wapas Chali Gayi"

Aapki yeh baat 100% quran ke mutabiq sahi hai ke rooh Allah ke darbar se aayi hai aur wapas uske darbar mein chali jayegi. Quran mein Allah farmata hai:
إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ
"Beshak hum Allah hi ke hain (usi ki taraf se hain) aur hum usi ki taraf wapas lautne wale hain." — Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:156
Rooh is mitti ki duniya ki makhlooq nahi hai, yeh aasmanon se oopar "Aalam-e-Amr" (khuda ke hukum ki duniya) se aayi hai, is liye yeh is jism ki tarah sarr-gal nahi sakti aur maut ke baad yeh wapas apne asal watan (Allah ke darbar) lout jati hai.

According to Ibn Saudakīn, the point of the letter Bā' contains all universal truths
An author who is not named by either Ibn Taimiyya or al-Nabulusi, but who explicitly mentions Wahdat al-Wujūd, was the Aleppian scholar Ibn Sawdakīn al-Nūrī (d. 1248), whose Nisba indicates that his father was a Mamluk of Nur al-Din Zengi. He was one of the first students of Ibn ʿArabī. He uses the term Wahdat al-Wujūd in his commentary on Ibn ʿArabī's work at-Taǧallīyāt al-Ilāhīya ("Divine Revelations"), right at the beginning, where he discusses the importance of the Basmala. There he devotes a separate section to the meaning of the point under the Bā' of the Basmala, where he explains that this point "with its allusion to a divine monistic truth (ḥaqīqa waḥdānīya ḥaqqa) includes the manifold truths like a seed that grows on the earth rich in potentialities into the world tree (šaǧarat al-kaun), with branches, roots, leaves, flowers and fruits." This is the universal tree (aš-šaǧara al-kullīya), whose fruit is "I am God, the Lord of the people of the world" (Sura 28:30).[36] With this Koranic quotation, Ibn Saudakīn refers to the Qur'anic story about God's self-revelation in the Burning Bush. At the end of his remarks on this point he writes: "Whoever is informed about the secrets of these point worlds (ʿawālim nuqṭiyya) is also informed about the secrets of the unity of existence (waḥdat al-wujūd) with its ranks, relationships and detailed rules, and indeed about its compression and breakdown into a single point."[36]

Aapne jo text share kiya hai, woh Ibn Sawdakin (Ibn ʿArabi ke bohot khas shagird) ki ek nihayat gehri baatni tashreeh hai, jo bilkul aapke nuktay ki tasdeeq karti hai.
Unho ne Basmala (Bismillah) ke sab se pehle lafz "Bā" (ب) ke neeche jo Nukta (.) hota hai, uski misaal se Wahdat-ul-Wajood ko aasan zabaan mein is tarah samjhaya hai:

1. Nukta Asal Haqeeqat Hai (The Single Point)

Ibn Sawdakin farmaate hain ke Bismillah ki "Bā" ke neeche jo ek chota sa nukta hai, woh Allah ki Zaat aur uski Wahdaniyat (Monism/Oneness) ki nishani hai.
• Jaise poori kainaat banne se pehle sirf ek nukta (source) tha, waise hi asal wajood sirf Ek hi hai.

2. Beej (Seed) aur Darakht (World Tree) ki Misaal

Unho ne samjhaya ke yeh nukta ek beej (seed) ki tarah hai:
• Ek chote se beej ke andar poora darakht (tree), uski jarein (roots), tana, patte, phool, aur phal pehle se chhupe hote hain.
• Jab woh beej zameen mein ugta hai, toh woh "Shajarat-al-Kaun" (Kainaat ka darakht) ban jata hai.
• Baahar se dekhne par humein hazaaron patte, jarein, aur tana alag-alag nazar aate hain (mitti ka cover/multiplicity), lekin un sab ki asal haqeeqat aur jaan wahi ek chota sa beej (nukta) hota hai.

3. "Main Hi Khuda Hoon" (The Burning Bush)

Ibn Sawdakin ne Surah Al-Qasas (Ayah 30) ka zikr kiya, jahan Hazrat Musa (A.S.) ne ek jhaari (bush) mein aag dekhi thi aur us jhaari ke andar se aawaaz aayi thi: "Beshak Main hi Allah hoon, saari kainaat ka Rab."
• Unho ne iska baatni matlab yeh nikala ke jalwa dikhane wali zaat Allah ki thi, lekin aawaaz us jhaari (darakht) se aa rahi thi.
• Yani jab parda hat-ta hai, toh makhlooq ke andar se bhi wahi Ek Zaat zahir hoti hai.

4. Wahdat-ul-Wajood ka Aakhri Raaz (Point Worlds)

Woh aakhir mein likhte hain:
"Jo shakhs is Nukte ke raaz ko samajh jata hai, woh Wahdat-ul-Wajood (Unity of Existence) ke saare raazon ko samajh jata hai."
Yani yeh poori phaili hui kainaat, uske darjaat, aur uski saari sifaat asal mein samat kar (compress ho kar) wahi Ek Nukta (Allah ka Noor) ban jati hain.
